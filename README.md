## Hi, I'm Gopalakrishna 👋

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer** at SunRay Construction Solutions in Hyderabad. I have seven years of experience in construction operations and research, and I build production-oriented AI systems that turn messy operational workflows into governed, reviewable intelligence.

My work covers **agentic AI, retrieval-augmented generation, LLM applications, document intelligence and explainable ML**. I design for source grounding, observability, human review and clear failure boundaries, not just impressive demos.
